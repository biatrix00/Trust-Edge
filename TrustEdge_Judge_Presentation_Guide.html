🏆 6. Top 5 Tough Judge Questions & Winning Answers

Q1: "Why do you need both Optical IR and Ultrasonic sensors? Isn't one enough?"
Answer: "No single sensor modality is secure. Ultrasonic sensors can be jammed with cheap speakers or sound-absorbing acoustic foam. Optical sensors can be blinded by sunlight or clear glass. By cross-arbitrating light waves against sound waves, an attacker must simultaneously spoof two completely separate branches of physics at the exact same microsecond."
Q2: "Why didn't you just use a standard Kalman Filter?"
Answer: "A Kalman Filter assumes Gaussian noise (random bell curve). But cyber-physical spoofing is malicious and non-Gaussian. If an attacker injects a lie, a Kalman Filter averages the lie into the truth, leading to catastrophic vehicle drift. TrustEdge uses deterministic physics gating: if a reading violates Newtonian mechanics, it is rejected with zero trust."
Q3: "What happens if Bluetooth disconnects while the rover is moving?"
Answer: "The Arduino firmware runs a dedicated hardware fail-safe watchdog (WATCHDOG_TIMEOUT_MS = 3000). If no valid command packet is received within 3 seconds, the microcontroller automatically cuts motor power. The car will never run away."
Q4: "How does this prevent the Tower Pro servo from buzzing or vibrating?"
Answer: "Over Bluetooth, SoftwareSerial disables global interrupts during transmission, which causes timing jitter on Timer 1 and shakes the servo. We engineered a 300ms Auto-Detach Engine: once the servo reaches target angle, the firmware automatically detaches it, shutting off PWM pulses and completely eliminating jitter and power drain."
Q5: "How does this scale up to real autonomous vehicles like Tesla or Waymo?"
Answer: "The exact same 3 pillars scale directly. In a full-size vehicle, Pillar 1 arbitrates LiDAR (light) against Radar (radio) and Cameras (photons). Pillar 2 enforces vehicle kinematics (v ≤ 130 km/h, a ≤ 1g). Pillar 3 detects frozen CAN bus packets. Our mathematical model provides the verifiable safety envelope that sits between AI perception and drive-by-wire actuators."
